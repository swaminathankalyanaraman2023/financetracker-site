Privacy policy

FinanceTracker is a personal tool used only by its owner's household. It is not available to the public.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, FinanceTracker reads emails in one dedicated Gmail inbox owned by the household, using the gmail.modify scope, to extract bill and payment amounts and due dates. It adds labels to processed emails. It does not send, delete, or forward email.

What it stores

Extracted amounts, dates, sender, subject, and a short redacted excerpt are stored in the household's private database. Full email bodies, full account numbers, and medical visit details are not stored.

Sharing

Data is not sold, shared, or used for advertising. Redacted email text may be processed by an AI service solely to classify the email. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removal

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account's security settings, and stored data can be deleted by the owner.
